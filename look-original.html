About RecoverWell

A home-based clinic in the heart of Rolleston

Happy to have you here!

RecoverWell is a home-based clinic located in the heart of Rolleston, Christchurch, providing professional massage therapy services from 25 Falcon Road.

We offer therapeutic and relaxation massage to clients for optimal recovery, whatever their goals may be. There is a particular focus on deep tissue work, myofascial release work and mobilisation to aid in recovery and injury rehabilitation.

Please feel free to have a look around, learn more about us and what we do, and book an appointment when you’re ready.

Services

What massage does RecoverWell offer?

Therapeutic and relaxation massage for optimal recovery, whatever your goals may be.

  • Therapeutic massage

    Hands-on treatment aimed at your recovery, shaped around whatever your goals may be.

  • Relaxation massage

    Time set aside to unwind and relax, part of your path to recovery and relaxation.

  • Deep tissue work

    A particular focus at RecoverWell. Deep tissue work targets the deeper layers of muscle to aid recovery.

  • Myofascial release

    Focused work on the fascia, the connective tissue around the muscles, to support recovery and rehabilitation.

  • Mobilisation

    Mobilisation work used alongside massage to aid in recovery and injury rehabilitation.

  • Injury rehabilitation

    Deep tissue, myofascial release and mobilisation work brought together to aid injury rehabilitation.
